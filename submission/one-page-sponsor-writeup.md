# alphadecay

Freeze the reason before entry. Check every later action against it.

## One position, reported by the broker

On September 3, alphadecay approved one SPY call debit spread after every recorded entry gate passed. The product submitted one multileg limit order through Alpaca at 10:45:02 AM ET, and the paper broker recorded a complete simulated fill. The spread expires October 9, 2026. Quantity was one and debit paid was $175. Whole account reconciliation certified the filled entry, and the spread became an open managed position. The media adds current account equity, market value, unrealized P&L, DTE, delta, and theta from a read only Alpaca check at render time. Strikes and every account, order, and position identifier stay private.

## The reason came before the action

The plan was frozen at 10:29:59 AM ET, before approval and before the broker request. It bound the bullish SPY thesis, the defined risk structure, and both option legs. This ordering matters: a reviewer sees what alphadecay was meant to test before seeing whether it acted. The same export counts every scheduled cycle, including cycles with no trade and checks that authorized no action, so the single fill is not presented without its base rate.

## AI logic

The model works inside a narrow classification boundary. It receives evidence IDs selected by the application and returns bounded labels for relevance, materiality, confidence, direction, structure readiness, and unanswered questions. It does not choose a source, calculate exposure, select a contract, set quantity, approve an order, call a broker tool, or write the explanation shown to a reviewer. Application code restores the cited facts around those labels. Fixed policy then evaluates the frozen thesis, current evidence, and allowed actions.

## Position management and risk gates

Fixed policy evaluates hold, close, or roll on a schedule. Since the fill, each scheduled check authorized no action, and the position stays open under its plan. The mandatory close is due September 4 at 9:45 AM ET. That is stated as intent; the record will show what happened. Missing facts mean no action. Before any write, the product checks the paper role, market session, exact spread, quote quality, buying power, inventory, earlier orders, and the approved loss envelope. Live trading, market orders, separate leg orders, naked shorts, and model approval are unavailable.

## Alpaca's three roles

The Trading API supplies the judged paper account state, order, simulated fill, positions, and reconciliation. The official MCP server supplies a small, application selected set of read only research calls; the model cannot direct MCP. The CLI stays outside the web app and production image and checks the paper endpoint. Public Replay is separate sample data. It never stands in for broker activity.

## Result and limitation

The result so far is one paper fill authored by the product and an open managed position. A close and realized P&L are not yet part of the record. The current equity and unrealized P&L are broker marks, not a realized result. Alpaca paper fills are simulated, and the free options feed is indicative rather than OPRA. One paper outcome does not establish an edge, live performance, or lasting returns.
